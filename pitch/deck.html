03
Who pays for missing trust

Three honest people, one broken paper trail.

The trader

“My receipt is in Chinese. My goods wait months. I carry cash over the pass.”

Small importer from Gilgit-Baltistan. Paper receipts, manual declarations, no record they can prove.

Customs at Sost

“Every file looks the same. I can’t tell which truck to open.”

Values and HS codes declared by hand. No way to tell a clean file from an edited one.

The honest seller

“Someone rewrote my invoice. My name is on the fraud.”

Kashgar wholesaler whose real price gets under-declared later in the chain.

What we built

One phone app from market stall to port gate.

1

Scan

AI reads the Chinese receipt, translates it, suggests 8-digit PCT codes, checks sums and value bands.

2

Manifest

WeBOC-style declaration built from the lines. Trader confirms each one.

3

Seal

Ed25519 signature made on the phone. A QR seal anyone can verify.

4

Transit

Signed GPS checkpoints, offline over the pass, synced at Sost.

5

Pay

Seller counter-signs amount. Double-entry sandbox ledger.

6

Clear

Risk-ranked console. Tamper shown to the exact line. Trust score grows.

06
Ten layers, one envelope

To forge a shipment you must beat all ten.

01Device keysEd25519 generated in the browser, non-exportable
02Canonical hashRFC 8785 JSON, same bytes in Python and JS
03Hash chainEvery event links to the previous one
04Published headServer counter-signs the chain head after each event
05Self-verifying QRSeal carries hash, signature, key fingerprint
06Replay guardNonce per event, device and server time both kept
07Append-only auditDatabase triggers reject updates and deletes
08Key revocationLost phone? Its later signatures stop counting
09PIN lockoutFive bad PINs locks the account; rate limits on API
10Safe uploadsMagic-byte check, size cap, re-encoded images
