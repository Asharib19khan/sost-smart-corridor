# Research facts — Sost Smart Corridor

Retrieved 2026-11-16 via web search. One fact per bullet: fact · source · publication date (where known) · confidence.
Confidence: **H** = official/primary or multiple consistent reports; **M** = single reputable outlet; **L** = conflicting/secondary. Only H/M facts are used in the deck.

## Corridor, volumes, revenue
- Customs collected ~Rs14.93 bn on Khunjerab imports in FY2025-26 vs Rs13.67 bn in FY2024-25 (Collector Customs GB, Wajid Ali); exports ~Rs2.8 bn. · [Dawn 1953421](https://www.dawn.com/news/1953421), [Pakistan Observer](https://pakobserver.net/?p=772886) · 2026 · **M**
- Record Rs9.5 bn collected at Sost in H1 FY2024-25 vs Rs6.5 bn a year earlier. · [Dawn 1799843](https://www.dawn.com/news/1799843/record-rs55bn-collected-from-sost-dry-port) · 2025 · **M**
- October 2025: Rs1.88 bn, highest monthly collection in the port's history. · [PakWheels](https://www.pakwheels.com/blog/?p=218151) · Nov 2025 · **M**
- Up to March 2026: 1,774 consignments from China cleared at Sost, Rs10.16 bn revenue. · [Dawn 1983245](https://www.dawn.com/news/1983245) · 2026 · **M**
- Khunjerab was seasonal (1 Apr – 30 Nov); moved to year-round operation, inaugurated for year-round trade 22 Apr 2025 (APP: from 1 Dec). Weekend closures still apply. · [Dawn 1906063](https://www.dawn.com/news/1906063), [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=903823) · 2024–2025 · **H**

## Clearance problems at Sost (2025)
- Traders demanded clearance of ~240 China consignments stuck at Sost for seven months (later reported ~280). · [Dawn 1925708](https://www.dawn.com/news/1925708/traders-block-sost-port-over-khunjerab-trade-halt) · Jul 2025 · **M**
- Sit-in halted Khunjerab trade and travel for 20+ consecutive days (by 8 Aug 2025), later ~50 days; trade suspended ~68 days in 2025. · [Dawn 1929586](https://www.dawn.com/news/1929586), [Dawn 1949453](https://www.dawn.com/news/1949453) · Aug–Oct 2025 · **M**
- Trade resumed after a customs appellate tribunal ruling on stuck consignments. · [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/10/17/trade-resumes-at-sost-dry-port-after-customs-tribunal-ruling-on-stuck-consignments) · 17 Oct 2025 · **H**
- PM approved exemption of income tax, sales tax and FED on Khunjerab imports by GB-domiciled residents, under a quota system. · [Dawn 1963116](https://www.dawn.com/news/1963116) · late 2025 · **M**

## Under-invoicing / mirror gap (China → Pakistan)
- PBC: 2013 China-reported exports to Pakistan $11.02 bn vs Pakistan-reported imports $6.62 bn — a $4.4 bn gap. · [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/28954/entertainment), [BR](https://www.brecorder.com/2018/03/12/404239/pak-china-under-invoicing/) · 2018 · **M**
- FBR (2023): real gap $2–3 bn, not $7 bn; residual after removing Afghan transit etc. ~$545 m. · [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2023/11/30/fbr-resolves-misreporting-of-pak-china-trade-gap/) · 30 Nov 2023 · **M**
- Customs (Oct 2026): mirror discrepancies alone are not conclusive proof of under-invoicing; FY2024-25 gap ~$6 bn attributed mainly to incomplete data capture. · [Tribune 2634691](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2634691/customs-rejects-under-invoicing-claims) · Oct 2026 · **M**
- Pakistan and China agreed electronic trade-data exchange (from 30 Apr 2018) to check mispricing. · [The News](https://thenews.com.pk/print/287689-pakistan-china-to-exchange-datafrom-april-30-to-check-trade-mispricing) · 2018 · **M**

## Digital customs in Pakistan
- WeBOC is FBR's web-based end-to-end clearance system incl. clearances from border customs stations. · [COMCEC/FBR](https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/9-TRD-PRE-PAK.pdf) · 2021 · **H**
- FBR is specifying a new customs digital management system to replace WeBOC; Sost listed under GB collectorate. · [FBR notification](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/20249161494231240NoticicationWorkStream.pdf) · Sep 2024 · **H**
- FBR began design phase (Mar 2026) of a Digital National Cargo Tracking System + e-Bilty, legal basis Finance Act 2025, ~1.5 years to implement; QR digital seal per consignment planned. · [Dawn 1978938](https://www.dawn.com/news/1978938/pakistan-moves-to-digital-cargo-tracking), [SLD](https://sld.com.pk/?p=2737) · 2026 · **M**

## Duties
- FY2025-26 budget replaced CD slabs 3/11/16% with 5/10/15%; max CD from 20% to 15%; ACD cut/abolished across thousands of lines. · [Nukta](https://nukta.com/pakistan-unveils-tariff-reforms), [ARY](https://arynews.tv/budget-2025-26-unveiled-by-muhammad-aurangzeb/) · Jun 2025 · **M** (line counts conflict — check Finance Act)
- RD capped at 20% from 1 Jul 2026. · [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/01/govt-caps-regulatory-duty-on-imports-at-20percent-from-july-1) · Jul 2026 · **M**
- Standard sales tax 18% (Sales Tax Act); WHT on imports under s.148 ITO. · general knowledge, not re-verified today · **L** — state only as "CD + ACD + RD + 18% ST + WHT", no rates per item.
- Pakistan Customs Tariff uses 8-digit PCT codes (HS 6 + 2 national). · FBR PCT · **H** (widely known; link FBR tariff when citing)

## Payments
- Raast: SBP instant payment system (via Raast Payments Pakistan, SBP subsidiary), launched 11 Jan 2021, PKR bank-account rail. · [SBP](https://www.sbp.org.pk/our-subsidiaries/raast), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raast) · **H**
- SBP allowed ICBC Pakistan local CNY clearing since 2015; ICBC designated RMB clearing bank by PBoC (Oct 2023); BOC and Standard Chartered also allowed CNY settlement setups. · [BR](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40270469/rmb-clearing-operations-milestone-achieved), [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/amp/1470735), [Dawn 1380403](https://www.dawn.com/news/1380403/door-open-to-yuan-based-trade-with-china-sbp) · 2018–2023 · **H**

## What we must NOT claim
- Not a number of Sost traders, trucks per day, or dollar trade value through Khunjerab — not found in a citable source.
- Not "$X bn under-invoicing through Sost": mirror gaps are national, all routes, and officially disputed.
- Not that we are integrated with WeBOC/PSW, approved by FBR/SBP, or licensed to move money. Sandbox ledger only.
- Not that GPS proves location; it is a consistency signal. Hardware e-seal is next phase.
- Not that AI HS codes are authoritative — they are suggestions an officer confirms.
- Not that Raast settles CNY — it is a PKR rail; CNY goes via clearing banks.
- Not specific duty rates per item without checking the current PCT/Finance Act.
- Not that RECTS-style time savings will repeat here.
- Unverified: cash-carrying volumes/robbery figures on KKH, GB chamber membership size, Sost clearance time in hours.
